About the Movie

John is astudent-athlete whose life is all about basketball. He can is very good on the court , but when it comes to romance, his emotional processing speed is very slow. In the first semester the arrival of two international exchange students namely Lisa and her best friend Michelle. While their friends Nii and Lisa hit it off instantly, John and Michelle are stuck in a cycle of mixed signals. John is so focused on his training that he misses all signs Michelle is giving him. With only 120 days until the girls leave, John must realize that his biggest win isn't on the court, but with the girl who has been supporting him from the shadows.

Meet the Cast

  • John : A student athlete with high atheletic physic and a slow romantic intuition.
  • Michelle: A shy, exchange student who observes the world from the sidelines.
  • Nii: Johns best friend and social guy who helps bridge the emotional gap.
  • Lisa: Michelle's best friend who enjoys every moment of the exchange program.
